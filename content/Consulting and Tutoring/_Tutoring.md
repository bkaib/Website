# 🧑‍🏫 Tutoring in Math, Statistics, Economics & Coding

Whether you're struggling with calculus, need help understanding probability,
econometrics or want to learn programming for data analysis, I offer personalised
support that adapts to your level and goals. With over a decade of tutoring experience up to university level, my aim is to make difficult concepts clear and applicable.

# 📚 Subjects Covered

- **Mathematics:** Everything up to German Abitur and entry University level. This includes for example algebra, calculus, linear algebra, differential equations and real analysis.
- **Statistics & Data Science:** probability theory, statistical inference, regression,
  time series, machine learning basics.
- **Economics:** microeconomics, macroeconomics, econometrics, game theory.
- **Coding & Tools:** Python, Machine Learning, Data Analysis & Visualisation

# 🧭 Tutoring Approach

1. **Assessment:** we start by identifying your strengths and pain points.
2. **Custom plan:** each session is tailored with examples, exercises and real-world applications.
3. **Interactive Study Design:** Lessons are interactive and driven by your questions. Bring your problems and questions to your tutoring session. This way you will learn quicker!   
3. **Resources:** Together we will develop notes and problem sets. You will have access to an online notebook with all your writings in one place (OneNote).
4. **Feedback:** I will give you feedback on your learning progress regularly. This way we can adjust the lessons to your own pace.
5. **Simulation of Exams:** If you want we can simulate the situation of exams. This way you can learn how to manage your time better and to compute under pressure.

Sessions are conducted online via video call. Scheduling is flexible, with evening and weekend slots available.
