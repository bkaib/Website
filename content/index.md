# What I'm Working On

1. **PhD in Climate Science:** Besides my graduations in higher mathematics, economics, and sports I studies Ocean & Climate Physics at the University of Hamburg. I finished my Masters Degree in 2022 and started my PhD-Position in 2023 at the [Helmholtz-Center Hereon](https://www.hereon.de). <a href="https://www.hereon.de" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer">Helmholtz-Center Hereon</a>
2. **Extreme Events and Their Physical Mechanisms:** In my PhD I focus on [[Weather and Climate Extremes]], their possible [Impacts](Impacts%20of%20Weather%20and%20Climate%20Extremes%20) and their underlying [Physical Drivers](Physical%20Drivers%20) with and emphasize on [[Wind Extremes]].

3. While studying, I started **Graduates**. We helped hundreds of students to
   understand math and economics in an easy way. Mainly we give private
   tutoring and online courses.

4. Just recently I started **@youcation**. Our goal is to incorporate SDGs into
   education and everyday life.

5. On my surf trips I take pictures along the way, mostly of landscapes or surfers.  
   Let me know if you like my favorite pictures!

6. On this site I write articles about my work in climate science, share my
   experience in surf photography, summarize books that I've enjoyed, and let you
   know how I am staying productive.
